# Whizi Field Guides

Short, practical guides on getting more out of AI models, written by Nour, founder of [Whizi](https://whizi.io/). Whizi puts 280+ AI models in one chat, so most of these guides come from watching what people actually do when every model is one click away.

Each page here is a condensed version. The full guides, with the tables, prompts and current prices, live on whizi.io and are linked from every page.
